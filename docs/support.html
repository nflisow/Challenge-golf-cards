Cards Against Golf Support

Thanks for playing Cards Against Golf! If you're having trouble with the app, have a question, or want to suggest a new challenge card, we're happy to help.

Contact Support

For questions, bug reports, or other support requests, email golfcardsforfun@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem, please include your device type, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Common Questions

The app isn't working correctly

Try completely closing Cards Against Golf and reopening it. If the problem continues, restart your device and make sure you're using the latest version of the app.

My cards or settings disappeared

Some game information is stored locally on your device. Removing the app, clearing its data, or changing devices may remove locally stored information.

I found a bug

Please email us and describe what happened. Screenshots are helpful when available.

I have an idea for a new card

We'd love to hear it! Email us with your idea for a challenge card or app improvement.

Privacy

About Cards Against Golf

Cards Against Golf is a golf challenge game designed to make rounds with friends more entertaining through challenges, custom cards, score tracking, and additional golf games.
